# Lift Log

A single-page workout tracker for a 4-day upper/lower hypertrophy plan with partner cardio twice a week.

**Live app:** https://beechbum12b.github.io/lift-log/

## Features

- Pick a week (1–5, week 5 is a deload) and a day (1–4) to see that session's exercises
- Log weight and reps for every set; each exercise shows what you did last time
- Enter your 1-rep maxes for bench, squat and deadlift to get weekly target weights
- Log partner cardio rounds on Days 1 and 3
- History of every saved session, newest first
- lb or kg

## Add it to your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen

## Where your data lives

Everything is stored in your browser on your own device (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and each person's log is private to their phone. Clearing the browser's website data will erase your history, so keep the app on your home screen and don't clear site data for it.

## Development

It's one file, `index.html`, with no build step or dependencies. Edit it and push to `main`; GitHub Pages redeploys automatically.
