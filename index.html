Lift Log

Units

1-rep maxes and units

Enter these to get exact targets for bench, squat and deadlift. Everything else is suggested from your last logged session, so log your first week honestly and the suggestions sharpen from there. No tested max? Use weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30) from a recent hard set.

People

Each person has their own history, maxes and units. Switch between them with the menu at the top.

Backup

Logs live only on this device. Export a backup now and then, and import it on a new phone to bring your history with you. Importing adds missing sessions and never deletes any.
